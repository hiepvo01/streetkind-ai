- If something is not mentioned at all, leave it false. But if the volunteer describes a CATEGORY (e.g. "drunk", "intoxicated", "wasted", "high", "stumbling", "out of it"), set the most representative sub-field(s) in that category to true.

  - "I'm helping...", "I noticed...", "we saw...", "we encountered...", "we found..." -> the volunteer found them on patrol. Use "skAmbassador" (the volunteer is an ambassador on patrol) NOT "self".
  - "generalPublic" -> a passerby/bystander pointed them out to the volunteer.
  - "police"/"venueSecurity"/"transportStaff" -> those services flagged the person to the volunteer.
  - Default for ambiguous "I helped someone..." cases: "skAmbassador". DO NOT default to "self".
- For "otherServicesInvolved", determine which emergency/security services were also present or called. Valid options: {other_services_keys}.
- The "incidentDescription" should capture what happened. The "incidentOutcome" should capture how it was resolved.
- For "site", map to one of: {site_keys}.

- "high", "on drugs", "tripping", "took something" -> drugUseSigns.observed=true. "Smelled weed", "saw needle marks", "white powder" -> drugUseSigns.visibleSigns=true. If they admitted it -> drugUseSigns.disclosed=true.

- "yelling", "abusive", "swearing" -> offensiveConduct.offensiveLanguage or offensiveBehaviour.

- "walked them to their hotel/home/Airbnb" -> escortedTo.accommodation=true AND safeSpace.escortedTo=true.
- "called their mate/family", "got them in touch with someone" -> reconnection.telephone or reconnection.person.
- "gave them water" -> basicAid.water=true. "vomit bag" -> basicAid.vomitBag. "thongs/shoes" -> basicAid.footwear. "lolly/lollipop" -> basicAid.lollipop.

- "called ambo/police/fire" -> emergencyServicesCalled.ambulanceServiceCalled / .policeServiceCalled / .fireServiceCalled=true.
