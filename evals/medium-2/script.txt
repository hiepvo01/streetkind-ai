Rangers radioed us just before midnight about an intoxicated bloke near IMAX
in a red hoodie who'd knocked back their help. We found him passed out — Cody,
eighteen, said he's from Oatley. He was in a real state: vomit in his hair and
on his clothes, foam around his mouth, soaking wet, slurring, totally
confused, couldn't walk without us holding him up. We walked him back to the
safe base and gave him water and a vomit bag. At first he reckoned he'd just
been drinking — vodka and tequila — but after a while he admitted he'd taken a
couple of caps and smoked some weed too. He was up and down, a few flashes of
aggression, kept shouting abuse at people walking past and trying to provoke
them, and we had to calm that down a few times. His mates had already gone
home so he was on his own. We found his phone in his bag and got his dad on
the line — his dad came and picked him up around two.
