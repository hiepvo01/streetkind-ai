Rangers radioed through just before midnight with a report of an intoxicated male in a red hoodie near IMAX who'd refused their assistance. We responded and discovered Cody, an eighteen-year-old from Oatley, passed out at the location. He presented with multiple concerning signs: vomiting visible in his hair and clothing, foam around his mouth, he was soaked through, his speech was severely impaired, he lacked coordination and balance, and he was disoriented. We transported him to the safe base where we provided water and a vomit bag. Initially he reported consuming only alcohol — vodka and tequila — but subsequently disclosed that he had also consumed capsules and cannabis. His presentation was marked by emotional volatility, including periodic aggressive outbursts directed at passers-by with offensive language and attempts at provocation, which required de-escalation on multiple occasions. His companions had departed the area, leaving him without peer support. We recovered his mobile phone and facilitated contact with his father, who arrived to collect him approximately two hours later, around two am.
