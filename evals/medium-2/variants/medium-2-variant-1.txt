So the rangers called us up around midnight saying they'd spotted this young bloke in a red hoodie near the IMAX who was pretty hammered and had turned them away. When we got there Cody — he's eighteen from Oatley — was completely passed out. The bloke was in a shocking state, honestly: had vomit all through his hair and all over his clothes, there was foam coming out of his mouth, he was soaking wet and barely able to stand let alone walk. We helped him back to the safe base, got some water into him and grabbed a vomit bag for him. Turns out he'd had vodka and tequila but when we talked to him more he confessed he'd also done a couple of caps and had a smoke as well. He was all over the place emotionally — angry one moment, then nothing — and kept yelling at random people going past, trying to start fights. We had to step in a few times to settle him down. His mates had bailed on him so it was just us and him. Found his phone, rang his old man, and the dad came and collected him about two in the morning.
