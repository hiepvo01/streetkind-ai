We got word from the rangers just before the midnight mark that there was an intoxicated bloke in a red hoodie near the IMAX who'd already turned down their help. When we found him, Cody — that's his name, eighteen, lives in Oatley — was passed out cold. The state he was in was pretty dire: hair and clothes covered in vomit, white foam at the corners of his mouth, completely soaked, his words were slurred and hard to understand, totally lost and disoriented, and he couldn't walk at all without our support holding him up. We took him to the safe base and provided him with water and a vomit bag. He initially said it was just alcohol — vodka and tequila — but after a bit he came clean that he'd also taken a couple of caps and some weed. He kept swinging between calm and really angry — aggressive moments where he'd shout foul language at people passing by and try to wind them up, and we had to de-escalate several times. His friends had already left him there on his own. We managed to get his phone and contacted his dad, and his dad picked him up around two in the morning.
