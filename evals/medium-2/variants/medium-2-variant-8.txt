The rangers flagged an issue just before midnight: there was a bloke in a red hoodie by IMAX, heavily intoxicated, and he'd knocked back their offer of support. We arrived on scene to find Cody, aged eighteen from Oatley, completely unconscious. He was in a bad state, to say the least: his hair and clothes had vomit on them, he had foam round his mouth, was soaking wet, had severe slurring, couldn't make sense of anything, and physically couldn't walk without our assistance. We escorted him to the safe base where we gave him water and a vomit bag. He said at first he'd only consumed alcohol — vodka and tequila — though he later told us he'd also taken a couple of caps and cannabis. He displayed a lot of instability throughout — aggressive episodes with offensive language and attempts to provoke bystanders — and we had to calm him multiple times. His friends had left him there by himself. His phone was located and his father was contacted via telephone and came to collect him around 2 am.
