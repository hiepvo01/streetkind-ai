Rangers called in around midnight about an intoxicated young man in a red hoodie near IMAX who'd refused their help. When we got there, Cody was completely out. He's eighteen, from Oatley. The lad was in a terrible state: vomit in his hair and all over his clothes, foam round his mouth, soaking wet, badly slurred speech, really confused, absolutely couldn't walk on his own without us holding him up. We got him to the safe base and gave him water plus a vomit bag. He told us he'd had vodka and tequila, but then admitted he'd also dropped a couple of caps and had a joint. He kept getting aggressive — yelling at people going past, swearing at them, trying to start something — and we had to settle him down quite a few times. His mates had gone home and left him alone. We got his phone, rang his dad on it, and his old man arrived around two to pick him up.
