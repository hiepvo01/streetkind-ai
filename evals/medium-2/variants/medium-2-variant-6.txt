Just before midnight the rangers radioed that they'd come across a young bloke near IMAX in a red hoodie, very intoxicated, who'd declined their help. We attended and found Cody unconscious — he's eighteen, from Oatley. He was clearly in distress: vomit matted through his hair and covering his clothes, foam visible round his mouth, he was completely soaked, barely coherent, terribly confused, and couldn't walk without us essentially carrying him. At the safe base we gave him water and provided a vomit bag. Initially Cody claimed he'd only been drinking — vodka and tequila — but he later admitted to also taking a couple of capsules and smoking cannabis. Throughout the night he was highly volatile: angry periods punctuated by aggressive language and attempts to harass and provoke pedestrians, which we had to intervene on several occasions to calm down. He was alone, as his friends had already gone. We found his phone, got through to his father, and his father came to pick him up around two o'clock in the morning.
