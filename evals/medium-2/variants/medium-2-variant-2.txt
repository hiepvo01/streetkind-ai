Got a call from the rangers just before midnight — they'd found this lad in a red hoodie by IMAX, absolutely out of it. He'd knocked back their offer of help. We arrived and found Cody, eighteen years old from Oatley, completely unconscious. Mate, he was in a bad way: vomit in his hair, vomit down his shirt, foam round his mouth, drenched through, couldn't string a sentence together, and needed us to physically support him to move. We brought him to the safe base, gave him water and a vomit bag. He said at first it was just booze — vodka and tequila — but then admitted he'd taken a couple of pills and smoked some weed. Throughout the night he was really unstable — moments of real aggression where he'd start screaming at people walking by and trying to pick fights, and we had to keep stopping him. His mates had left him there alone. We located his phone, got hold of his dad, and his dad picked him up around two o'clock.
