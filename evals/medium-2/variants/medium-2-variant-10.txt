Rangers radioed us just before the stroke of midnight: they'd spotted a very drunk bloke in a red hoodie near the IMAX and he'd rejected their assistance. We got to the location and found Cody — eighteen years old, Oatley resident — completely passed out. The bloke was in a shocking condition: vomit throughout his hair and clothing, foam at his mouth, soaked to the skin, speech heavily slurred and difficult to follow, extremely disoriented, unable to stand or walk without physical support from us. We transported him to the safe base, supplied him with water and a vomit bag. Initially he reported consuming only alcohol — vodka and tequila — but then disclosed that he'd also consumed a couple of caps and cannabis. He exhibited significant behavioural instability: aggressive outbursts involving shouting abusive language at passing pedestrians and deliberate attempts at provocation, which necessitated our intervention on multiple occasions. His peer group had departed and he was unaccompanied. We secured his phone and established contact with his father via telephone, who collected him at approximately 2 am.
