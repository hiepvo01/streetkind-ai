Got a call from the rangers just before midnight about a young guy in a red hoodie near IMAX who was very drunk and hadn't accepted their help. We made it to the spot and Cody was lying there unconscious. Cody's eighteen, he's originally from Oatley. He looked awful: vomit was in his hair and on his clothes, there was foam around his mouth, he was completely wet through, his speech was really hard to understand, he was confused, and he needed us to support him just to stand. We brought him into the safe base and gave him some water and a vomit bag. He said he'd been drinking — vodka and tequila — but then he told us he'd also taken a couple of caps and smoked weed. He was really unstable — getting aggressive, yelling abuse at random people walking by, trying to start fights — and we had to step in and calm him down a number of times. His mates had already taken off on him. We found his phone, called his dad, and his dad picked him up around two in the morning.
