Around half twelve we spotted a young guy, Elias, outside Cargo Bar with a
couple of female friends. At first I thought he was just drunk — a bit
unsteady, slurring — but talking to him there was a lot more going on. He was
having a panic attack, hyperventilating, and told me straight out that he
wanted to die, that he "wants to go to heaven." He said his meds weren't
working, and he admitted he'd been harming himself lately. He also said he
didn't want to go home because he's scared of his dad — sounded like there's
violence at home. He seemed a bit drug-affected as well, not just alcohol. I
sat with him, got him some water, and did grounding and breathing exercises to
bring him down. His brother came out of the bar and helped. Once Elias settled
we got him and his friends into an Uber to a friend's place in Menai.
