We came across Elias just after half twelve—he was out the front of Cargo Bar with a couple of his mates, women they were. First impression was alcohol, you know, unsteady on his feet, slurred speech. But when you actually talk to someone like that you start picking up on other stuff. Turns out he's having a panic attack, breathing all wrong, hyperventilating. He just told me he wants to die, that he wants to go to heaven. Medication's not doing the job, he says. Plus he let on that he's been self-harming. And here's the thing—he's scared to go home because of his old man, and from what he said it sounds violent. I could tell he was on something other than just booze too. So I sat with him, gave him some water, worked through breathing exercises and grounding techniques to help him calm down. His brother turned up and lent a hand. After he'd settled a bit we got him and the girls sorted into an Uber heading to a mate's place out in Menai.
