You could tell straight off he'd had a lot to drink—couldn't balance properly, slurring his words everywhere. But having an actual chat with him showed us it was much worse than just being drunk. He's hyperventilating, having a panic attack, and he comes straight out and says he wants to die, wants to go to heaven. Meds aren't helping him. He also let us know he's been self-harming recently. And get this—he doesn't want to go home because he's terrified of his dad and there's violence there. Plus you could tell he'd used other stuff beyond just alcohol. So I sat with him, gave him water, and we did breathing exercises and grounding techniques. His brother showed up and helped. Once he'd calmed down we got him and the girls into an Uber heading to a place in Menai.
