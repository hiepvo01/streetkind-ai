Around half twelve we found Elias outside Cargo Bar with a couple of girls he knew. At first glance he seemed wasted—wobbly, couldn't speak clearly—but when you actually listen to what he's saying, there's serious stuff underneath. Panic attack, can't breathe right, hyperventilating. He comes out and tells me he wants to die, that he wants to go to heaven. Says his medication isn't doing anything for him. And he's been self-harming. He doesn't want to go home because he's scared of his father—and there's violence involved. I also got the sense he was on more than just alcohol. Gave him water, worked through some breathing and grounding with him to help him settle. His brother came out and helped support him. After he'd gotten a bit better we sorted him into an Uber with his friends to go to someone's place in Menai.
