Spotted Elias outside Cargo Bar about half twelve with a couple of girls. He seemed drunk at first—unsteady, slurred speech—but when I spoke to him it became obvious there was a lot more. The guy was hyperventilating, full-blown panic attack. He told me he wanted to die, wanted to go to heaven. Said his meds weren't working. Admitted to recent self-harm. Also mentioned he's frightened of his dad and there's violence happening at home. Appeared drug-affected too, not just alcohol. I got him water, did some grounding and breathing work with him. His brother helped out. We managed to get him into an Uber with his friends—they went to a mate's place in Menai.
