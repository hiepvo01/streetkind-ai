We came across Elias around 12:30 outside Cargo Bar. He was with a couple of female friends. He looked intoxicated—unsteady, mumbling—but when we spoke to him it was clear there was a lot more going on. He was hyperventilating, in the middle of a panic attack. He told us he wanted to die, wanted to go to heaven. His medication wasn't working. He'd also been harming himself, which he disclosed to us. And he said he couldn't go home because he was afraid of his dad—sounds like there's violence in that house. He seemed drug-affected as well, not just drunk. I got him some water and we did breathing and grounding exercises with him. His brother showed up and helped. Once he'd settled a bit we got him and his friends into an Uber headed to a mate's place in Menai.
