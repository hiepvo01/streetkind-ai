Around 12:30 we identified a young bloke named Elias outside Cargo Bar. He was with a couple of female companions. Initially appeared to be drunk—lost his balance easily, slurring his words—but there was clearly much more happening. He disclosed active suicidal thoughts—specifically said he wants to die, wants to go to heaven. He was also in the middle of a panic attack, hyperventilating heavily. He mentioned his medication isn't working and he's been hurting himself. Also revealed he's afraid of his father and that there's violence at home. Drug-affected beyond just the alcohol. I provided water and engaged him with breathing techniques and grounding exercises. His brother came out and supported. Once he stabilised we arranged transport by Uber with his mates to a friend's house in Menai.
