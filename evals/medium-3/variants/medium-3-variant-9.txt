Young guy called Elias was standing outside Cargo Bar about 12:30 alongside a couple of female friends. He was clearly drunk—stumbling, slurred words—but the more you talk to him the more you realise it's not straightforward. He's in the midst of a panic attack, hyperventilating badly. He confessed he wants to die, wants to go to heaven. His medication isn't working. He confessed he's been harming himself. He's also terrified of his dad and there's domestic violence at home. Drug-affected beyond just alcohol too. I gave him water and we did breathing techniques and grounding exercises. His brother turned up and lent support. We got him and his mates into an Uber destined for a friend's place in Menai.
