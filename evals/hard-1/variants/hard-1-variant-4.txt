At the Rocks safe base, late night around eleven, a young woman showed up — Maya, looked to be in her early twenties. She was having trouble with her balance, stumbling around, and when she sat down she basically fell into the chair hard. Her speech was really slurred, and her behaviour was all erratic and aggressive — lots of swearing, crying, telling us all to get away from her. I was pretty sure there was drug use on top of the drinking because her pupils were massive and she was doing this teeth grinding thing the whole time. The combination of signs made it clear it wasn't just alcohol. We got her some water. Since she was alone and didn't have mates around, we charged her phone up and got one of her friends on the line to collect her. Later she mentioned that her cousin had said she hit her, and earlier we'd actually seen the pair of them pushing each other — she had a fresh scratch down her arm from the scuffle. She also shared that she has bipolar, anxiety and PTSD, and she was clearly struggling mentally. We stayed close and kept her as calm as we could manage. The police arrived eventually, she was okay to go with them, and they brought her over to the Rocks police station.
