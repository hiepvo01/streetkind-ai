Around eleven PM we were at the Rocks safe base when Maya, a young woman in her early twenties, came over to us in quite a state. She was completely unsteady on her feet, stumbling constantly, and just dropped into a chair when she tried to sit. Her speech was heavily slurred and incoherent, and mentally she was all over the map — swearing, crying, getting aggressive, telling us to leave her be. On top of the obvious intoxication I was pretty convinced she'd used something — her pupils were dilated right out and she had this constant jaw clenching, teeth grinding thing going on. The physical signs and behaviour screamed drug use as well as alcohol. We made sure she had water. She didn't have her friends with her, so we plugged her phone in to charge and rang up one of her mates to come get her. Later she told us that her cousin had accused her of hitting her, and we'd seen them shoving each other earlier in the night — she had a fresh scratch mark on her arm from it. She disclosed she has bipolar, anxiety and PTSD and was really struggling, so we sat with her and made sure she stayed as calm as we could keep her. Police turned up in the end, she was willing to go with them, and they took her to the Rocks police station.
