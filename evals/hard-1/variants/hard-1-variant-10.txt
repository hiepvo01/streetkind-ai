Late evening at the Rocks safe base, approximately eleven o'clock, a young woman named Maya, appearing to be in her early twenties, presented to us in a significantly compromised state. Balance and coordination were clearly affected — stumbling, difficulty maintaining upright posture, she collapsed into a chair. Her speech was heavily slurred and difficult to understand, and her mental state was chaotic — aggressive language, swearing, crying episodes, hostile demands for people to leave her alone. Beyond the alcohol intoxication, there were clear indicators of drug use: markedly dilated pupils and persistent teeth grinding. The constellation of signs pointed to polysubstance use. We offered her water. She was unaccompanied, so we assisted with charging her phone and successfully connected her with a friend by telephone. Subsequently, she disclosed an accusation from her cousin of hitting her; we had earlier witnessed a physical altercation between them, and she bore a fresh scratch on her arm as evidence. She disclosed diagnoses of bipolar disorder, anxiety and PTSD, and was experiencing distress. We provided supportive presence and maintained a calm environment around her. Police responded and she consented to go with them to the Rocks police station.
