So we were at the Rocks safe base, it was around eleven o'clock at night. This young woman called Maya, early twenties, she came up to us and she was totally out of it. Couldn't really stand properly — stumbling everywhere — and when she tried to sit down she literally just toppled into the chair. Her speech was all slurred, really hard to understand, and she was acting chaotic. She was swearing her head off at us, crying one minute, then telling us to get lost, just real aggressive stuff coming out of her mouth. I reckon it wasn't just the alcohol though — her eyes were massive, proper dilated pupils, and she was clenching her jaw constantly, grinding her teeth. That behaviour looked like she'd taken something on top of the drink. We got some water into her. She didn't have any of her mates with her, so we helped get her phone charged up and managed to get one of her friends on the phone to come pick her up. Then later on she mentioned that her cousin had said she hit her, and we'd actually witnessed them shoving each other earlier — she had this fresh scratch running down her arm from it. She also opened up about having bipolar, anxiety and PTSD and that she was really struggling mentally. We stayed with her, kept her as calm as we could. Eventually the cops arrived and she was willing to go with them, so they escorted her over to the Rocks police station.
