At eleven o'clock that night at the Rocks safe base, a young woman in her early twenties turned up. Her name was Maya and she was in a terrible state — heavily intoxicated and unsteady, stumbling everywhere, collapsed into a chair when she went to sit down. Her speech was completely slurred and her behaviour was all over the place — aggressive, lots of swearing and crying, telling us to leave her alone. I suspected drug use alongside the drinking based on her hugely dilated pupils and constant teeth grinding. The way she was acting and her physical state made it clear it wasn't just alcohol. We provided her with water. She didn't have any friends with her, so we charged her phone and managed to contact one of her friends to come collect her. She then disclosed that her cousin had accused her of hitting her, and we'd observed them pushing each other earlier — she had a fresh scratch on her arm from the altercation. She also shared that she has bipolar, anxiety and PTSD and was struggling significantly. We remained with her to keep her calm. Police arrived and escorted her to the Rocks police station after she agreed to go with them.
