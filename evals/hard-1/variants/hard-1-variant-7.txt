Just after eleven at the Rocks safe base, this young woman came up — Maya, early twenties. She was heavily intoxicated, couldn't stand steady, kept tripping over herself, fell right into the chair when she sat. Speech totally slurred, couldn't make out half of what she was saying. Behavioural-wise she was a mess — lots of aggression, swearing, crying, telling people to back off. I'm pretty sure there were drugs involved too, not just drink. Her pupils were massively dilated, constant jaw clenching and teeth grinding. Behaviour and physical signs definitely suggested something more than alcohol. We gave her water to drink. She was on her own, no friends around, so we helped charge her phone and got through to one of her friends to come get her. She mentioned later that her cousin had accused her of hitting her, and we'd witnessed them shoving each other beforehand — she had a fresh scratch on her arm from it. She disclosed she's got bipolar, anxiety and PTSD, which she was struggling with quite badly. We stayed with her and kept her as settled as we could. The police came and she agreed to go with them, and they walked her over to the Rocks police station.
