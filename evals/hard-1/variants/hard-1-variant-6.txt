This young woman named Maya showed up at the Rocks safe base, must've been around eleven at night. She was in her early twenties and she was really messed up. Unsteady, falling around the place, couldn't even manage to sit down properly — just toppled into the chair. Slurred speech, all confused and scattered, and her attitude was aggressive the whole time — cursing at us, one minute she's crying, next she's yelling at us to back off. It was more than just alcohol though, I reckon. Her eyes were huge, pupils blown out, and she kept grinding her teeth. Those signs said drugs to me, alongside the booze. We gave her water. She was flying solo, no mates there, so we got her phone charged and managed to get hold of one of her friends to come pick her up. She told us that her cousin had said she hit her, and we'd seen them having a punch-up earlier — left her with this fresh scratch down her arm. She also let us know she's got bipolar, anxiety and PTSD and she was really in a bad place mentally. We kept her company and tried to keep her relaxed as much as we could. When the police came around, she went along with them, and they escorted her down to the Rocks police station.
