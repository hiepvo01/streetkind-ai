Around half past ten at the Rocks safe base, a young woman in her early twenties — Maya was her name — turned up looking in a pretty bad state. She was unsteady, falling into the chair when she went to sit, speech was all over the place, completely slurred. She kept swearing, crying, telling everyone to back off — the whole thing was aggressive and chaotic. Beyond the alcohol, I suspected she'd been using other drugs: her pupils were absolutely dilated, she was grinding her teeth constantly. The behaviour and physical signs pointed to more than just booze. We gave her water and some space. She was by herself, no friends around, so we helped her charge her phone and got through to one of her mates to come collect her. She then disclosed that her cousin had accused her of hitting her, and we'd actually seen the two of them having a physical altercation earlier — she had a visible scratch on her arm from that. She also told us about her bipolar diagnosis, anxiety and PTSD, and she was struggling quite a bit. We sat with her and did what we could to keep her stable. Police showed up after a bit, she agreed to go with them, and they took her to the Rocks police station.
