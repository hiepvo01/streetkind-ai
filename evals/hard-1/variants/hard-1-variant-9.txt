We were working the Rocks safe base around eleven at night when Maya, a young woman in her early twenties, came over to us looking really unwell. She was all over the place physically — stumbling, unsteady — and she fell straight into the chair. Her speech was massively slurred and she wasn't making much sense. Behaviourally she was a nightmare, swearing constantly, crying, aggressive, telling everyone to leave her alone. But looking at her, it wasn't just alcohol — her pupils were huge and she was grinding her teeth non-stop. Those physical signs plus her behaviour made it pretty obvious there were drugs involved. We got some water for her. She didn't have any mates with her, so we got her phone charged and rang one of her friends to come get her. Later she said her cousin had accused her of hitting her, and we'd already seen them in a physical row — she had a fresh scratch down her arm from that. She also told us she has bipolar, anxiety and PTSD and she was struggling quite a lot with it. We stuck with her and did our best to keep her calm. Police turned up and she went with them, and they took her to the Rocks police station.
