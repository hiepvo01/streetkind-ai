It was late, around eleven at the Rocks base. This girl, Maya, early twenties, came over to us and was clearly wasted. Couldn't keep her balance — kept stumbling about — and collapsed into a chair. Her speech was slurred and garbled, and she was all over the place mentally. Swearing at us, crying, being aggressive, yelling at people to leave her alone. But there was more to it than just alcohol, yeah? Her pupils were huge, she was clenching her teeth the whole time. Those signs made me think she'd taken something else too. We got her some water. She was on her own, no one with her, so we got her phone charged and managed to reach one of her friends to come get her. She mentioned her cousin had said she'd hit her, and before that we'd seen them actually shoving each other — she had this fresh scratch down her arm from it all. She also told us she's got bipolar, anxiety and PTSD, and she was in a bad way emotionally. We just sat with her and tried to keep her as calm as possible. Then police turned up and she went with them without much fuss, and they walked her to the Rocks police station.
