Okay, this was at the Rocks safe base, about eleven at night. A young woman —
early twenties — came up to us at the base, her name was Maya. She was really
unsteady on her feet, kept stumbling, and when she went to sit down she just
fell into the chair. Her speech was slurred and she was all over the place —
swearing at us, crying, telling everyone to leave her alone, really aggressive
with her language. On top of the drink I'm pretty sure she'd taken something —
her pupils were huge and she was grinding her teeth, her behaviour looked like
more than just alcohol. We gave her some water. She was on her own, her mates
weren't around, so we helped her charge her phone and got one of her friends
on the line to come get her. A bit later she told us her cousin had accused
her of hitting her, and earlier we'd actually seen the two of them shoving
each other — Maya had a fresh scratch down her arm from it. She also told us
she has bipolar, anxiety and PTSD and was really struggling, so we sat with
her and tried to keep her calm. Eventually the police turned up and she agreed
to go with them, and they walked her over to the Rocks police station.
