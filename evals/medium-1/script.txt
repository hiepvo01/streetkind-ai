The rangers came and got us — there was a woman collapsed inside the
convenience store across from the Argyle. When we got there she was on the
ground, so we put her in the recovery position. Her husband was with her,
already on the phone to triple zero. He told us she'd taken a heap of pills on
purpose — a whole box of cold-and-flu tablets, No-Doz, Panadol, a couple of
types of Nurofen — that she'd run off from home and done it to try to end her
life. He said she's got a long history of mental health issues and past
suicide attempts. She was barely responsive, non-verbal, but she'd look at you
if you said her name. Her pulse was up and her breathing quick. She started
vomiting so we gave her a vomit bag. We stayed with her and followed the
triple-zero operator's instructions until the ambulance arrived and took her
and her husband to St Vincent's.
