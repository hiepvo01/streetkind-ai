A woman had collapsed in a convenience store opposite the Argyle, and the rangers responded first and called us in. Her husband was there. He'd already rung emergency. Told us that his wife had deliberately taken a lot of tablets - whole box of cold-and-flu, No-Doz, Panadol, different types of Nurofen. Suicide attempt. She has a history of mental health issues and past attempts. She was barely conscious, non-verbal, but responsive to her name when spoken. Fast heart rate, rapid breathing. She was sick, we gave her a vomit bag. We positioned her in recovery and stayed with her, following the triple-zero operator's guidance until the ambulance got there and transported her and her husband to St Vincent's.
