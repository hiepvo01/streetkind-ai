Got dispatched to the Rocks, convenience store right across the Argyle. Rangers had found a woman down. We put her in recovery position once we got there. Her husband was with her and had already phoned triple zero. He said she'd intentionally taken a massive amount of pills - cold-and-flu tablets, No-Doz, Panadol, various Nurofen - she'd bolted from the house trying to end her life. She's dealt with mental health issues long-term and has attempted suicide before. She was barely reactive, couldn't speak, but she'd respond if you called her name out. Pulse elevated, breathing rapid. She threw up, we gave her a vomit bag. We placed her on her side and followed the triple-zero operator's instructions until the ambulance arrived and took her and her husband to St Vincent's Hospital.
