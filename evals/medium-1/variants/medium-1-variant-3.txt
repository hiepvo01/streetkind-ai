Got called out to a convenience store across from the Argyle. Rangers had found a woman collapsed inside. Her husband was there, he'd already called triple zero. Said his wife had deliberately overdosed - box of cold-and-flu tablets, No-Doz, Panadol, couple of different Nurofen products. She wanted to end her life. Long history of mental health issues, previous attempts. When we arrived she was basically unresponsive, couldn't speak, but you could get her attention by using her name. Her pulse was quick and her breathing rapid. She vomited while we were there so we gave her a vomit bag. We put her in recovery position and stuck with her, following the triple-zero operator's instructions the whole time. Once the ambulance turned up they took her and her husband to St Vincent's.
