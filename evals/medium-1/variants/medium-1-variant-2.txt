Rangers got called to a convenience store opposite the Argyle in the Rocks, and they brought us in. A woman had collapsed in there. We positioned her properly on her side for recovery, and her husband - he was already on the phone to emergency when we arrived. He told us what happened: she'd taken a whole box of various tablets intentionally. We're talking cold-and-flu tablets, No-Doz, Panadol, multiple types of Nurofen. She was trying to end things. Long history of mental health stuff, previous suicide attempts. She was barely conscious, non-verbal, though she'd react if you said her name. Vital signs were concerning - pulse racing, breathing shallow and quick. Started vomiting, we got her a vomit bag. Stayed put, following the operator's guidance till ambulance arrived and transported her and her husband to St Vincent's Hospital.
