The rangers found a woman collapsed in a convenience store across from the Argyle and we were called to assist. Her husband was present and had already contacted triple zero. He disclosed that she'd taken a deliberate overdose - a complete box of cold-and-flu tablets plus No-Doz, Panadol, and a couple of different Nurofen formulations - with the intention of ending her life. She has a longstanding mental health condition and has previously attempted suicide. Upon our arrival she was barely conscious and couldn't speak, though she would respond when her name was called. Her vital signs showed an elevated heart rate and rapid breathing. She vomited during our care, so we provided a vomit bag. We placed her in recovery position and stayed with her following the triple-zero operator's instructions until an ambulance arrived to transport her and her husband to St Vincent's Hospital.
