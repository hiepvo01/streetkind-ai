So the rangers called us out to the Rocks - convenience store right across from the Argyle. Woman was on the ground, collapsed. We got her into recovery position straight away. Her husband was there with her and he'd already rung triple zero. Turns out she'd deliberately taken a whole pile of tablets - cold-and-flu stuff, No-Doz, Panadol, different Ibuprofen products - basically tried to kill herself. He mentioned she's had mental health problems for ages and had attempted it before. She wasn't really conscious, couldn't speak, but if you called out her name she'd respond to it. Her heart rate was elevated, breathing was fast. Then she started being sick, so we gave her a vomit bag and kept her stable, doing what the triple-zero operator told us until the ambulance showed up. They took her and her husband off to St Vincent's.
