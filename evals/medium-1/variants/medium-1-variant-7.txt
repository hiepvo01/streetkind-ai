Rangers brought us to a convenience store across from the Argyle where there was a woman on the ground. Her husband was there, had called triple zero already. He told us his wife had taken a whole box of tablets deliberately - cold-and-flu stuff, No-Doz, Panadol, multiple Ibuprofen products. She'd run away from home and done it trying to take her own life. Long history of mental health issues and other suicide attempts in the past. When we got there she was barely conscious, non-verbal, though responsive to her name. Raised pulse, quick breathing. She vomited, so we got her a vomit bag. We put her in recovery position and maintained her until the ambulance arrived - they took her and her husband to St Vincent's.
