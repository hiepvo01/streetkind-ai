The rangers had us respond to the Rocks - convenience store just across from the Argyle. Woman down inside. We got there, put her in recovery position. Her husband was beside her, already on the line with triple zero. He explained she'd taken heaps of pills on purpose - whole box of cold-and-flu stuff, No-Doz, Panadol, a couple of different Nurofen types - attempting suicide. She's got a background of mental health conditions and has tried before. She was barely alert, couldn't speak, but would look at you when you said her name. Elevated heart rate, fast breathing. She started vomiting, we provided a vomit bag. We stayed with her and followed the triple-zero operator's directions until the ambulance came and took her and her husband to St Vincent's Hospital.
