Alright, so there was a woman collapsed in a convenience store across from the Argyle - rangers had spotted her and called us in. Her husband was right there with her, he'd already rung triple zero. The story was she'd intentionally taken a heap of medication - cold-and-flu tablets, No-Doz, Panadol, a couple variants of Nurofen - suicide attempt. Long history of mental health conditions and prior attempts. She was pretty much non-responsive when we arrived, couldn't talk, though she'd acknowledge you if you used her name. Her pulse rate was up, breathing quick and shallow. Vomited at one point, so we handed her a vomit bag. We placed her on her side for recovery and stayed with her, doing what the triple-zero operator told us to do until the ambo arrived. Took her and the husband to St Vincent's.
