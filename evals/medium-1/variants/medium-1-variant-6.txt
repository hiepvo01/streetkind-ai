We responded to a convenience store in the Rocks, opposite the Argyle. Rangers had reported a woman collapsed inside. Her husband was present and he'd already contacted emergency services. According to him, she'd taken a deliberate overdose of over-the-counter medication: entire box of cold-and-flu tablets, No-Doz, Panadol, and a couple of different Nurofen products. This was a suicide attempt. She has an extensive background of mental health problems and has made previous attempts. Her state when we arrived: barely conscious, non-verbal, but she showed responsiveness to her name. Physical observations: elevated pulse, rapid breathing. She experienced vomiting and we gave her a vomit bag. We placed her in recovery position and remained with her, adhering to the triple-zero operator's instructions until an ambulance arrived. The ambulance transported her and her husband to St Vincent's Hospital.
