We were on patrol near King Street Wharf when we noticed a couple having a
really nasty row. The woman — Tara — was clearly wasted, stumbling, slurring,
couldn't coordinate herself at all, behaving erratically. She looked
drug-affected too, had that glassy, wired look. She was shouting and swearing,
being pretty offensive to people walking past. Then it turned physical — we
actually watched her partner grab her and shove her hard into a wall, and she
had a split lip and bruising coming up on her cheek. And it didn't look like
the first time — she had older yellowing bruises on her arms, the kind you'd
associate with ongoing domestic violence. We called it in to CCTV and got the
rangers over, and we stayed with Tara to keep her safe and calm.
