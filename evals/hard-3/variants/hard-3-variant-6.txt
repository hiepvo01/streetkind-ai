Patrol took us to King Street Wharf and we spotted what looked like a real argument between a couple. Tara was extremely drunk—she could barely walk, words were coming out garbled, she was clumsy and all over the place. Added to that, she had that look of someone who'd used drugs—glassy, wired eyes. She was being verbally abusive, yelling and swearing at people going past. Then the partner got physical—we saw him grab her and drive her hard into a wall. Split lip appeared, bruising came up on her cheek. What was concerning was the older bruises on her arms, yellowing, which told the story of sustained domestic violence. We called it in to CCTV, brought the rangers in, and stuck with Tara to make sure she stayed safe and managed to relax.
