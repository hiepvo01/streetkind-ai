During our patrol around King Street Wharf, we witnessed a couple in a heated dispute. Tara, who was with her partner, was significantly intoxicated—exhibiting slurred speech, poor balance, and loss of coordination. She displayed characteristics of drug use as well, with a glassy and wired demeanor. She was engaging in offensive behavior toward others, shouting and using obscene language. The situation became physical when her partner violently shoved her into a wall. Tara sustained a split lip and developing bruises on her cheek. She also had older bruises on her arms with a yellowed appearance, suggesting a chronic pattern of domestic violence. We alerted CCTV and the rangers immediately, then remained with Tara to provide safety and support until she could calm down.
