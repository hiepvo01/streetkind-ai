During patrol we came across a situation at King Street Wharf—a couple arguing, and it was getting pretty heated. Tara, the woman involved, was clearly intoxicated—couldn't balance properly, slurred speech, you could see the lack of coordination. She was also showing signs of drug use, had that distinct wired, glassy look about her. She was becoming abusive verbally, shouting and swearing at people. Then it turned violent right in front of us—her partner shoved her forcefully into a wall. She ended up with a split lip and cheek bruising. Looking at her arms, there were older bruises in various stages of healing, which suggested ongoing domestic violence. We immediately alerted CCTV, got the rangers to come down, and made sure we stayed close to Tara to keep her calm and protected.
