We were doing a patrol near King Street Wharf when we noticed a couple having this massive argument. The woman—Tara—was really intoxicated, stumbling, her speech was slurred, she couldn't coordinate properly at all. She looked drug-affected as well, that typical glassy and wired appearance. She was shouting, swearing, really offensive to people around her. It turned physical when her partner grabbed her and pushed her hard into a wall. She had a split lip that opened up and bruises forming on her cheek. There were also older bruises on her arms, yellowing ones, which you could tell were from ongoing domestic violence. We rang CCTV, got rangers to attend, and stayed there with her to keep her safe and try to calm her down.
