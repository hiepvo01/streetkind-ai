We were patrolling the King Street Wharf area when we encountered a couple having a significant altercation. The woman, Tara, displayed clear signs of intoxication—unsteady gait, slurred speech, impaired coordination. She also appeared drug-affected with glassy, wired eyes. Her behavior toward passersby was offensive, involving shouting and profanity. The situation became violent when her partner grabbed and forcefully pushed her against a wall. She presented with a fresh split lip and emerging cheek bruises. On examination, older yellowing bruises were visible on her arms, indicating a history of domestic violence. We contacted CCTV and requested ranger assistance, then maintained our presence with Tara to ensure her safety and emotional stability.
